## Enter

Somewhere between a confession and a performance, this website lives. *Circus of Love* is a scroll-driven, cinematic editorial piece — an original love letter dressed in the costume of a circus, where the ring is love, the audience is the world, and every performer is simply someone who dared to stay themselves under the spotlight.

It isn't meant to be read the way you read an article. It's meant to be *walked through* — one act at a time, one scroll at a time, the way the curtains part slowly rather than all at once.

> *"There were never any performers, only people brave enough to love."*

---

## 🎭 Six Acts, One Curtain Call

The letter unfolds the way a show would — announced, performed, questioned, and finally, understood.

| Act | Title |
|---|---|
| I | The Letter |
| — | The Ringmaster Announces |
| III | The Performance |
| IV | The Reflection |
| V | The Revelation |
| VI | The Final Letter |

Each act carries its own weight, its own light, its own silence — building toward a final truth: *pride is not a prize, it's a refusal to be ashamed.*

---

## 🕯️ The Look & Feel

This isn't just written — it's staged.

- **Typeface:** Bodoni Moda, EB Garamond, and Cormorant Garamond — serif voices that feel like ink on parchment, not pixels on a screen
- **Palette:** faded gold against parchment, like a program left behind after the show has ended
- **Motion:** spotlights that bloom as you scroll, curtains that part and close on cue, backgrounds that invert like a held breath — every transition timed to the emotional beat of the act it belongs to

---

## 🛠️ Built With

- **React** + **Vite** — for the frame the show is built on
- **Tailwind CSS v4** — for the parchment and gold
- **[Motion](https://motion.dev)** — for the curtains, the spotlight, the breath between scenes
